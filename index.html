About fabbery

Fabbery is the creator name I use for my work, and I want it to become associated with high-quality toys. I’m a young engineering student exploring 3D modeling and design, with a focus on creating durable, sturdy and structurally considered pieces made to scale to series production. Each one is designed to make a great gift, feel good to play with and look great on display
